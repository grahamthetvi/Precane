How to Build Your Precane

Building a push-lawnmower style PVC precane is a simple DIY project that requires basic tools. Here is a general guide to assembling your 11-piece frame using the dimensions calculated above.

Materials Needed:

  • Schedule 40 PVC Pipe (Use the diameter recommended in your results for the frame)
  • Oversized PVC Pipe (For the two rolling pieces, usually one size larger than the frame)
  • 6x PVC 90-degree elbows (2 for the handle, 4 for the base corners)
  • 2x PVC Equal Tree (Tee) Joints (to connect the main shafts to the base branches)
  • PVC Cement (optional, for permanent assembly)
  • PVC Cutter or Hacksaw
  • Measuring Tape and Marker
  • Sandpaper (to smooth rough edges)

Instructions:

1. Measure and Cut: Using the calculated dimensions, cut your PVC pipe into the 11 required pieces. You'll need the Handle (A), two Main Shafts (B & C), four Base Branches (D, E, F, G), two Axles (H & I), and two larger Rollers (J & K).

2. Assemble the Base: Start by sliding the Roller pipes (J & K) over the Axle pipes (H & I) so they can spin freely. Attach a 90-degree elbow to both ends of each Axle. Then, insert the four Branches (D, E, F, G) into the other side of these elbows, creating two separate "U" shapes. Finally, connect the front and back "U" shapes using the two Equal Tree (Tee) joints in the middle.

3. Attach the Shafts and Handle: Insert the two Main Shafts (B & C) into the top of the Tee joints on the base. At the top of the Shafts, attach the remaining two 90-degree elbows facing inwards, and connect them together using the Handle (A). The result should resemble a push lawnmower.

4. Test and Adjust: Have the user try the cane. The handle should rest comfortably near the hip or waist, and the frame should roll easily on the ground. If it feels too long or short, adjust the cuts of the Main Shafts (B & C) now.

5. Final Assembly: Once satisfied with the fit, you can use PVC cement to permanently glue the joints (being extremely careful NOT to glue the J & K rollers, which must spin freely). If you prefer to keep the cane adjustable or easy to disassemble for travel, leave it friction-fit (unglued) and ensure the joints are pressed together tightly.
